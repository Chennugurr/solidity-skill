# Asset And Settlement

- Define the enforceable claim represented by one token and who owes it.
- Reconcile token supply against custodial or registry records.
- Define mint evidence, redemption request, burn timing, settlement finality, fees, and failed settlement.
- Disclose custodian, issuer, transfer agent, upgrade, freeze, seizure, and redemption authority.
- Define behavior during custodian failure, asset impairment, legal dispute, chain outage, or contract migration.
- Separate technical auditability from legal validity and financial assurance.
