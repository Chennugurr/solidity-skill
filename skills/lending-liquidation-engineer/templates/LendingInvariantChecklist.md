# Lending Invariant Checklist

- [ ] Debt shares reconcile to total debt after every accrual
- [ ] Supply claims stay within assets and declared loss allocation
- [ ] Healthy positions cannot be liquidated
- [ ] Borrow and collateral withdrawal preserve required health
- [ ] Liquidation is capped by debt, collateral, and close factor
- [ ] Oracle failure cannot create free borrowing or unfair liquidation
- [ ] Bad debt, reserves, fees, and dust reconcile
