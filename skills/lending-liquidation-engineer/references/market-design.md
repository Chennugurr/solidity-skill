# Lending Market Design

- Define isolated versus pooled risk and who can add assets.
- Accrue interest before any operation that reads or changes debt or supply shares.
- State rounding direction for borrow, repay, withdraw, liquidation, reserve, and fee conversions.
- Prevent collateral withdrawal and new borrowing when post-action health is below the required threshold.
- Define utilization extremes, rate caps, reserve capture, minimum debt, and dust.
- Model fee-on-transfer, rebasing, callback, paused, and nonstandard tokens.
- Define bad-debt recognition, socialization, insurance, reserve use, and shutdown.
