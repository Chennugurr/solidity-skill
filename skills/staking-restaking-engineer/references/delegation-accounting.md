# Delegation And Accounting

- Define whether shares represent pooled stake, operator-specific stake, or a claim on a withdrawal queue.
- Update exchange rates, rewards, fees, and slashes before minting or burning shares.
- Prevent operator self-delegation, reward donation, or timing from stealing other delegators' value.
- State operator registration, commission, capacity, key rotation, removal, and redelegation rules.
- Separate protocol custody from validator or service custody.
- Avoid loops over all delegators or queued withdrawals.
