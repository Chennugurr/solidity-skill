# Stablecoin Design Checklist

- [ ] Stability and redemption model classified
- [ ] Liabilities and backing assets reconciled
- [ ] Mint, burn, redeem, fee, and reserve authority explicit
- [ ] Oracle and collateral risk limits defined
- [ ] Redemption liquidity and queue behavior defined
- [ ] Depeg, reserve shortfall, and shutdown behavior defined
- [ ] Offchain custody and legal claims disclosed
- [ ] No guarantee of peg, backing, or redemption implied
