# Stability Models

- Fiat or RWA-backed: redemption depends on custody, attestations, banking access, legal claims, and operator controls.
- Crypto overcollateralized: stability depends on oracle quality, liquidation capacity, collateral liquidity, and debt ceilings.
- Market or liquidity facilities: peg modules depend on inventory, fees, caps, and counterparty assets.
- Algorithmic or reflexive backing: model death spirals, endogenous collateral, bank runs, and governance intervention explicitly.

For every model, publish liabilities, backing composition, mint authority, redemption path, fees, caps, and emergency powers.
